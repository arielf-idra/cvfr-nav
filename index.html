CVFR Nav

A moving-map assistant for VFR pilots flying the CVFR routes in Israel (Israel only): the routes with the altitude published for your direction, airspace, hand-over points, NOTAMs for your flight's time, instruments the device can honestly measure, and flight recording with an approach debrief. An assistance tool, not a certified navigation system.
