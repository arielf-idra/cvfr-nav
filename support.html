CVFR Nav — Support

CVFR Nav is a moving-map assistant for VFR pilots flying the CVFR routes in Israel (Israel only). It is not a certified navigation system: fly with the current official charts and AIP, and follow ATC instructions.

Contact: cvfr.support@idra-tech.io

Before writing: Settings → AIP data shows the chart edition on board. If the map says the data is out of date, update the app from the App Store.

Reporting a data error: say which point, route, frequency or area, what the app shows and what the official publication says, with its edition and page. Aeronautical data in the app comes only from the Civil Aviation Authority of Israel's publications; corrections are checked against them.

Reporting an app problem: a recorded flight helps — Flights → the flight → Share flight, and attach the .cvfrflight file.

Recording cockpit audio from a Bluetooth headset: choose the headset in Settings → Cockpit audio. A Lightspeed Zulu sends the intercom over Bluetooth only while it is plugged into the aircraft's powered intercom socket — on battery alone, away from the aircraft, the recording is silent (Voice Memos is silent too). Check it in the aircraft with the avionics on.
