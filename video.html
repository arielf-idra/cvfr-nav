CVFR Nav Video

A Windows program for flight instructors and pilots who fly with CVFR Nav: it turns a flight's GoPro footage into a debrief video, with the app's moving map in a corner, the cockpit audio, the videos the pilot filmed, chapters for YouTube and, when the app made one, its approach debrief — all on the flight's own clock. The video is made on the pilot's computer. If the pilot wants, it is uploaded to the pilot's own YouTube channel (Private, Unlisted or Public, the pilot's choice).
