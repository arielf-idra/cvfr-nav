CVFR Nav — Privacy Policy

Effective: 1 October 2026

CVFR Nav is a moving-map assistant for VFR pilots in Israel. This policy covers the version of the app on the App Store.

What the app collects

Nothing. The developer receives no data from the App Store version of CVFR Nav: no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your position, from the device's location services, is used on the device to draw the map and raise alerts. It is not sent to the developer.
  • Recorded flights (track, altitude, the device's motion and barometer readings, events) and, if you switch it on, cockpit audio are saved in the app's own storage on your device. They leave it only when you share or export a flight yourself, to the destination you choose.
  • Photos of the sky for the cloud-cover estimate are analysed on the device and not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Settings and flight plans are stored on the device.

Services the app contacts

  • NOTAM feed — the app downloads the public NOTAM list from arielf-idra.github.io (GitHub Pages). The request carries no information about you or your position; like any web request it reaches GitHub with your device's IP address (GitHub's privacy statement).
  • Edition file — the app reads which edition of the CAA chart is currently published from arielf-idra.github.io/cvfr-nav/edition.json (GitHub Pages), to tell you when its data is out of date. The request carries nothing about you or your position.
  • Radio reports — while the app is open or recording a flight, it asks once a minute for the runway, wind and QNH read from public air traffic radio, from the developer's isr-atc service (llhz-now.vercel.app, hosted by Vercel). The request names only the airfield or unit; it carries nothing about you or your position. Like any web request it reaches the host with your device's IP address (Vercel's privacy policy).
  • Apple Maps — the map background and the place name shown when you tap the map come from Apple's map services, under Apple's privacy policy.

Test builds (TestFlight)

Pre-release builds given to invited test pilots send recorded flights to the developer while "Send recordings to the developer" is on in Settings — on by default in those builds, and the tester can switch it off: the track, the sensor log, the settings the flight was recorded with, crash reports, the name the tester enters, and the cockpit audio only if its own switch (off by default) is on too. The App Store version cannot send anything. They are stored in Apple's CloudKit for the developer to find problems, and deleted on request.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes and contact
